MaZhi guide

Contents: create the Google project · add your devices · stay signed in (automatic sync)

Create MaZhi's Google project

MaZhi has no server: your data goes into a folder of your Google Drive. To let it write there, Google requires a free “project”. No credit card. It takes about ten minutes, ideally on a computer.

Only one person of the duo follows this guide, with their Google account. The other person has nothing to do: MaZhi gives you an invite link to send them.

1. Create the project

  1. Open console.cloud.google.com/projectcreate.
  2. Project name: mazhi. Leave “Organization” on “No organization”.
  3. Click Create, then select the mazhi project at the top of the page.

2. Enable the two APIs

  1. Open Google Drive API and click Enable.
  2. Open Google Picker API and click Enable.

3. Consent screen

  1. Open Google Auth Platform and click Get started.
  2. App name: MaZhi. User support email: yours.
  3. Audience: External. Contact information: your email. Accept the terms, then Create.
  4. In Audience (left menu), under “Test users”, click Add users and add your Gmail address and your partner's. The app starts in “Testing” mode: only these addresses can sign in. To sync on its own without signing in again, see Stay signed in.

4. Sign-in credential

  1. In Clients (left menu), click Create client.
  2. Type: Web application. Name: MaZhi web.
  3. Authorized JavaScript origins: add MaZhi's address, without the path.
  4. Leave “Redirect URIs” empty, then Create.
  5. Copy the Client ID (it ends with .apps.googleusercontent.com).

5. API key (to pick the folder)

  1. Open Credentials, then Create credentials → API key.
  2. Open the new key:
    • Application restrictions: Websites, then add the same address followed by /*.
    • API restrictions: Restrict key, tick Google Picker API.
  3. Save, then copy the key.

6. Project number

On the dashboard, “Project info” card, copy the Project number (digits only).

7. In MaZhi

Paste the three values in the “Set up Google” screen, sign in and create the space. MaZhi then gives you the invite link for your partner. These values are not secret: they only work from the address you allowed.

“Google hasn't verified this app” shows up on first sign-in: that's normal for a project in Testing mode. Click “Continue”.

Add your devices (phone, tablet, another computer)

  1. On a device where MaZhi already works: Settings → Devices and invitations → Add one of my devices, then Copy or Share.
  2. Open the link on the new device and Sign in with Google with your own account.
  3. Tap Join a shared space, then Continue as …. Your profile, history and API keys are there.

For another person, use Invite someone instead: they create their own profile and add their own API keys. Created a profile by mistake? Settings → People and devices → Merge into…

Stay signed in (automatic sync)

By default Google signs MaZhi out every hour; the next tap anywhere signs it back in. To sync fully on its own, for months, do this once (about 10 min). Only the person who created the Google project does it. The Google Cloud pages may be in French: the French names are in brackets.

1. Branding page (Branding)

Google won't let you publish until this page is complete. Open Google Auth Platform → Branding and fill in:

  • App name (Nom de l'application): MaZhi.
  • User support email (Adresse e-mail d'assistance utilisateur): pick your Gmail address in the list. This is the field that is usually missing.
  • App logo (Logo de l'application): leave it empty. Uploading a logo forces a Google verification.
  • Application home page (Page d'accueil de l'application): MaZhi's address
  • Privacy policy link (Lien vers les règles de confidentialité): privacy page
  • Terms of service link (Lien vers les conditions d'utilisation): terms page
  • Authorized domains (Domaines autorisés): your domain (already there if you followed step 4).
  • Developer contact (Coordonnées du développeur): your email.

Click Save (Enregistrer).

2. Data access page (Accès aux données)

Open Data access, click Add or remove scopes (Ajouter ou supprimer des niveaux d'accès), paste https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive in “Manually add scopes”, then Add to table, Update and Save. If drive.file is still listed, you can leave it or remove it.

This is the full Drive permission: it is what lets each person read the files the other one writes in the shared folder (the narrower drive.file does not). Google labels it “restricted” and may show a “verification” notice: ignore it. For a personal app (under 100 users) no verification is needed; each person just clicks through the “unverified app” screen once.

3. Clients page (Clients)

Open Clients and click MaZhi web:

  • Authorized redirect URIs (URI de redirection autorisés): Add URI, paste MaZhi's exact address, then Save.
  • Client secrets (Codes secrets du client): copy the secret. If you can't see it anymore, click Add secret and copy the new one.

4. Audience page (Audience): publish

Open Audience and click Publish app (Publier l'application), then confirm. The status becomes In production. In “Testing”, Google would sign you out every 7 days. Test users don't matter anymore: without the shared folder, nobody else can see anything.

5. In MaZhi

  1. Settings → Stay signed in: paste the secret, Save, then Sign in once on this device.
  2. On each other device (yours or your partner's), the secret arrives on its own at the next sync: open Settings → Stay signed in and tap Sign in once on this device.

Each device then keeps a renewal key with the same Drive permission as above (MaZhi itself only touches its own folder). Sign out of Google in Settings removes it, and you can also revoke MaZhi any time from your Google account (Security → Third-party connections). Privacy: see privacy.html.
